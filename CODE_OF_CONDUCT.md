# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and players of every level, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (**Security → Report a vulnerability** works for private reports too, or open a minimal issue asking to be contacted). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders.
